# Instructions for Sponsors: Marking Your Selections

## 📝 How to Mark Your Selected Options

When you receive a Walgreens campaign content document, you'll need to mark which options you prefer for fields like Email Subject Lines and Banner Headlines.

---

## ✅ **Method: Add [X] Text Markers**

For any field with multiple options (1., 2., 3., etc.), add `[X]` at the beginning of the line for your selected option.

### Before (Unmarked):
```
EMAIL SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS
1. Consider Joining a Vitiligo Patient Registry
2. Your Experience with Vitiligo Matters—Join the WeConnect Registry
3. Learn About Research Opportunities for Vitiligo
```

### After (Marked):
```
EMAIL SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS
[X] 1. Consider Joining a Vitiligo Patient Registry
[ ] 2. Your Experience with Vitiligo Matters—Join the WeConnect Registry
[ ] 3. Learn About Research Opportunities for Vitiligo
```

---

## 📋 **Step-by-Step**

### 1. Open Your Word Document

The document will have sections like:
- **EMAIL SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS** (GREEN)
- **BANNER HEADLINE OPTIONS** (GREEN)
- **HERO SPACE IMAGE OPTIONS** (GREEN)

### 2. For Each GREEN Section with Options:

**If you see multiple numbered options:**
1. Position your cursor at the very beginning of your preferred option
2. Type `[X]` (including the brackets)
3. Add a space after `[X]`
4. For other options, type `[ ]` (empty brackets) if you want to be explicit

**Example:**
```
BANNER HEADLINE OPTIONS
[X] 1. Join a Patient Registry for Adults Living with Vitiligo
[ ] 2. Vitiligo: Empower Yourself with Knowledge and Care Options
[ ] 3. Support Vitiligo Research
[ ] 4. Help us advance Vitiligo treatment
```

### 3. For Single-Paragraph Sections:

**No marking needed!** Sections like these extract all text automatically:
- STUDY INFORMATION
- ELIGIBILITY
- WHAT TO EXPECT
- CLOSING

Just leave them as-is.

### 4. Save the Document

Once you've marked all your selections, save the document.

---

## 🎨 **What the Colors Mean**

| Color | Meaning | What to Do |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **GREEN** | Variable content - you choose | Mark selections with [X] if multiple options |
| **RED** | Walgreens standard - not editable | Don't modify these sections |

---

## 📊 **Which Fields Need [X] Markers?**

### ✅ Mark These (Multiple Options):
- **EMAIL SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS**
- **BANNER HEADLINE OPTIONS**
- **HERO SPACE IMAGE OPTIONS** (if multiple)

### ⏭️ Don't Mark These (Single Content):
- **STUDY INFORMATION** (full paragraph)
- **ELIGIBILITY** (full paragraph)
- **WHAT TO EXPECT** (full paragraph)
- **CLOSING** (full paragraph)
- **CTA 1** / **CTA 2** (usually single lines)

### ⏭️ Skip These (Walgreens Standard):
- **SALUTATION** (RED - standard content)
- **END MATTER** (RED - standard content)

---

## ⚠️ **Important Notes**

### Multiple Selections
You CAN select multiple options by marking several with `[X]`:
```
EMAIL SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS
[X] 1. Consider Joining a Vitiligo Patient Registry
[X] 2. Your Experience with Vitiligo Matters
[ ] 3. Learn About Research Opportunities
```
**Result:** Both options 1 and 2 will be extracted.

### No Selections
If you forget to add `[X]` to any option:
- ⚠️ You'll receive a **warning alert**
- ⚠️ The system will use the **first option as default**
- 📝 Recommendation: Go back and add `[X]` to your preferred option

### Text Format
The `[X]` must be typed as **plain text** (not a checkbox form control).

**Correct:** `[X]` typed with keyboard
**Incorrect:** ☑ form checkbox (these don't get extracted)

Once you've marked your selections:
1. ✅ Save the document

3. ✅ They will run the extractor
4. ✅ Your selections will be extracted to Google Sheets
5. ✅ JSON will be generated for the email platform

---

## ❓ **Questions?**

- **Q: What if I want to change a selection later?**
  - A: Just move the `[X]` to a different option and re-extract

- **Q: Do I need to remove the other options?**
  - A: No! Keep all options. Just mark your preferred one(s) with `[X]`

- **Q: Can I use checkboxes instead of [X]?**
  - A: No, the extraction tool reads text, not form checkboxes. Always use typed `[X]`

- **Q: What if there's only one option?**
  - A: No need to mark it! Single options are extracted automatically
